# Panjaz Website Design Directions

## Three Possible Approaches

### Canal House Heritage
**Very Brief Intro:** An editorial Panamanian restaurant identity with a deep burgundy canopy, ivory paper texture, fine gold rules, and restrained tropical linework. It feels warm, storied, and built for a modern neighborhood restaurant.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Market at Dusk
**Very Brief Intro:** A saturated, food-forward direction with sun-baked citrus, cobalt tile, and energetic hand-painted type. It prioritizes the immediate appetite appeal of street-market abundance.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Contemporary Tropic Atelier
**Very Brief Intro:** A quiet, gallery-like dining identity mixing large-format food photography, cool stone neutrals, and architectural negative space. It feels precise and premium without looking formal.

**Probability:** 0.08

## Selected Direction: Canal House Heritage

### Design Movement
This site adopts **New Latin American editorial hospitality**: a contemporary restaurant experience grounded in regional craft, travel-poster composition, and the architectural rhythm of Panama’s canal-side neighborhoods.

### Core Principles
1. **Ingredient-led warmth:** Food photography is treated as the proof of the brand, with generous crops and confident placement.
2. **Heritage without nostalgia:** Fine ornamental details, gold rules, and archival-paper texture suggest history while typography stays crisp and current.
3. **Asymmetric storytelling:** The site avoids generic centered blocks; major sections use an off-center editorial split, visually leading visitors from the brand story to the food and visit details.
4. **Consistent visual rhythm:** Burgundy, ivory, navy, and warm gold recur as structural anchors, not as decoration.

### Color Philosophy
Warm ivory functions as the restaurant’s welcoming tablecloth and keeps food colors vivid. **Panjaz Burgundy** carries appetite, confidence, and the menu-system continuity; charcoal navy adds refinement and depth; a restrained warm gold serves as the invitation line that helps people navigate the page.

### Layout Paradigm
The page reads as a sequence of framed scenes: a broad hero card offset against a paper field, a horizontal “Build Your Plate” dining strip, an editorial menu preview, and a final “Visit Panjaz” panel. Each scene has its own composition, rather than repeating a central card grid.

### Signature Elements
1. A burgundy, gold-edged arch plaque borrowed from the menu headline system.
2. Low-contrast tropical botanical linework that emerges from the page edges.
3. Gold route-lines and small diamond markers used to lead the eye across content.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are direct and intentional. Navigation uses smooth in-page anchors; menu and visit calls-to-action have tactile press states; images respond with subtle scale and shadow shifts rather than novelty effects.

### Animation
Sections reveal with a short 220ms upward fade and 50ms stagger. Botanical linework remains still. Buttons use a 140ms press scale and gold underline sweep. All motion is disabled or minimized when a visitor has reduced-motion preferences enabled.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** provides the expressive restaurant wordmark and selected editorial headlines. **Barlow Condensed** carries large all-caps menu labels and calls-to-action. **Manrope** is reserved for readable body copy, address information, and practical visit details. Display type is never used for dense utility text.

### Brand Essence
**Panjaz brings vivid Panamanian comfort food to guests who want a generous plate and a place with character.**

**Personality:** warm, proud, vivid.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct, sensory, and quietly confident. CTAs feel like restaurant invitations rather than generic conversion prompts.

> “Start with rice. Build the plate you came for.”

> “Pull up hungry. Leave with a Panamanian story.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The wordmark pairs a burgundy PANJAZ signature with a compact gateway-and-wave emblem. In the website header, it appears as a simplified emblem and wordmark lockup, so it remains legible at any screen width.

### Signature Brand Color
**Panjaz Burgundy — #6C0E12.**

## Style Decisions

All page and component styles must reinforce the Canal House Heritage philosophy. The site will use the existing premium menu artwork as a visual foundation and will not introduce generic purple gradients, default Inter typography, repeated pill UI, or excessive centered-card layouts.

The **gateway arch is the primary Panjaz shape** and will recur in hero framing, captions, badges, and key section identifiers. Food imagery is treated as editorial: warm, generous, richly textured, and intentionally cropped. The header wordmark remains visibly prominent, with the gateway-and-wave emblem functioning as a true restaurant anchor.
